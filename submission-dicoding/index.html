Hideo Kojima Separate from Sony

Hideo Kojima is continuing to build his studio outside Sony following the end of their publishing partnership. Kojima Productions is now focusing on its own projects, giving the studio more freedom over how its games are developed and released especially the new Physint project.

Microsoft Announce Plans Disc to Digital

Microsoft is making it easier for Xbox players to move their physical game collections into the digital library. The new disc-to-digital feature lets owners of supported Xbox One and Xbox Series X games claim a digital license by simply inserting the game disc into their console.

Graveyard Keeper 2 is Released

The wait is over. Graveyard Keeper 2 is officially out now, bringing players back to its weird medieval world. This time, players can manage their graveyard, rebuild the town, automate production with zombies, and even lead an undead army into battle.

The sequel is available now on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2.

Silent Hill: Townfall

Silent Hill: Townfall bringing a new first-person horror story to the series. Players take the role of Simon Ordell, who returns to the mysterious island of St. Amelia in Scotland.

The game introduces a new CRTV device that helps Simon uncover clues and detect threats while exploring the fog-covered town. It will be available on PS5, Steam, and Epic Games Store.

Control: Resonant

Control Resonant is coming on September 24, 2026, bringing players back into Remedy Entertainment's strange supernatural world. This time, players take control of Dylan Faden as he explores a distorted version of Manhattan while searching for his missing sister, Jesse.

The sequel introduces new combat abilities, a shapeshifting weapon called the Aberrant, and a mysterious new form of resonance. It will launch on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store.
